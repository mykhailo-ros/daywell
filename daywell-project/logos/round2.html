A · App icon symbols

A1

Horizon

Kept from round 1, refined

Same idea, tighter drawing. The sun is a touch smaller and the line a touch heavier and wider, so the mark sits more firmly at icon size and holds up as a one-colour shape.

Daywell

Works because

  • Calmest and warmest option
  • The strongest silhouette at 16px
  • Doesn't depend on any letter or language

Watch out

  • Sunrise marks are common in wellness, so it needs a distinctive wordmark beside it
A2

D with a sun

Your suggestion

A capital D with a real sun inside it, with eight short rays. The day holds one bright thing. Below 24px the rays merge into a blur, so the small version swaps the sun for a plain dot (shown at 20px and 16px).

Daywell

Works because

  • The most literal reading of “Day”
  • The rays give a friendly, optimistic tone
  • Works as a system: the same D can start the wordmark (wordmark C below)

Watch out

  • Needs two drawings, a full one and a small one
  • The rays add detail the rest of the UI doesn't have
A3

D with a rising sun

Horizon + D combined

A merge of the two directions you kept. The D frames a small horizon with the sun coming up over it. It reads as a letter from a distance and as a sunrise up close.

Daywell

Works because

  • Joins both favourites into one mark
  • Still a single solid shape, so it works in one colour
  • Pairs with Horizon used on its own, for example in illustrations-free headers

Watch out

  • The inner horizon is fine detail: it starts to fill in at 16px
A4

DW

Monogram

Both initials in the same rounded stroke. The W is drawn as two soft U-curves, more like a gentle wave than a sharp zigzag, so the pair stays calm.

Daywell

Works because

  • Spells out Day + well
  • Rounded strokes match the app's check-in rings
  • The wide shape suits a website header

Watch out

  • Two letters get small inside a square icon
  • Monograms feel more corporate than the other options
A5

DW with a sun

Monogram + sun

The W is lowered into two soft hills with the sun rising between them: “day” over “well”. It's the most story-like of the five.

Daywell

Works because

  • Tells the name as a tiny scene
  • The sun carries the accent in one clear spot
  • The W with its sun could also stand alone as a symbol

Watch out

  • The W no longer reads strongly as a letter
  • Wide marks lose size inside a square app icon
