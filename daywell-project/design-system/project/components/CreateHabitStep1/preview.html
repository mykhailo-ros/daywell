Create habit, step 1

The user names the habit, then picks a suggested chip to turn it into a concrete action.

  • Suggestions come from the name: a length of time, an amount, or a smaller version. "Study" becomes "Study for 30 minutes".
  • Only one chip can be selected. Write your own replaces the chips with a text field.
  • The "Your habit" preview is the line that appears in Today. The name stays as the label in My habits.
  • Next is enabled once there's a name and an action.
