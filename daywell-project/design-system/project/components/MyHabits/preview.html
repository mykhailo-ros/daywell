My habits

All active habits grouped by routine, each with its schedule and next due date.

  • The ··· menu has Pause, Edit and Archive. Paused habits move to a "Paused" group at the bottom and their streak is frozen, not lost.
  • Archive removes a habit from the active list but keeps its history.
  • "Ideas to turn into habits" holds saved intentions. Tapping one opens Create habit (10) with the name filled in. Save an idea opens Quick capture (09).
  • The Weekly review entry turns to "Ready" on Sunday evening; until then it shows the week so far.
