How it works

One list for the day. One tap to check in.

Daywell does three things: it organises your day, reminds you at the right moment and keeps track of your progress.

  1. 1 · Organise

    Everything for today, in one place

    Habits, a scheduled meditation and your workout appear together in time order, so you only have one list to look at.

  2. 2 · Check in

    Done, Skip or Reschedule

    One tap marks it done. Check in on Home or in the section and it updates in both places. Reminders arrive only when you've asked for them.

  3. 3 · Track

    Streaks, badges and a weekly review

    See what's working. Every Sunday, a short review suggests one small change, such as moving a habit to a time that suits you better.

Meditation

Find a session. Do it where it lives. Log it here.

Browse meditation, breathwork and affirmations by intention (Peace, Focus, Sleep, Energy) and by length. Start the session on its source, then come back and tap Done.

  • Your word is enoughCompletion is self-reported. No timers and no proof.
  • One gentle nudge, at mostIf you don't come back, you get a single notification asking if you finished.

Daywell doesn't host or play sessions. It helps you find them, schedule them and keep track.

Streaks

Streaks that forgive a busy day.

Daywell encourages you and never makes you feel guilty. These are the rules, and they're the same everywhere in the app.

Done

Counts, and adds a day to your streak.

Skip or Reschedule

Keeps your streak. It doesn't add a day and it doesn't break it.

Missed

Not checked off by midnight. The streak starts again, quietly.

Not scheduled

Days a habit isn't planned don't count at all.
